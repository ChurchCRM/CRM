# ChurchCRM 7.8.4 Release Notes

ChurchCRM 7.8.4 focuses on making everyday ministry tasks faster and more intuitive—from reviewing year-end giving and managing deposits to navigating on phones and tablets, coordinating volunteers, and sending family communications.

---

## ⚠️ Important Guidance Before You Upgrade

Please keep these workflow changes in mind when updating:

* **Legacy Volunteer Opportunities Retired:** The older Volunteer Opportunities (V1) tool is now hidden for churches that aren't actively using it. If your church still has legacy data, ChurchCRM will display a clear warning and guide you through converting it to the modern Volunteer Management experience.
* **Stricter Member Portal Sign-In & Verification:** Family verification emails now direct members straight to the Member Portal with clearer instructions, and member sign-in now requires exact email matching to keep household accounts secure.
* **Database Version Check:** The setup and upgrade wizard now stops early if your server is running an unsupported legacy database (such as MariaDB 10.0), preventing partial or failed upgrades.

---

## 💰 Finance, Giving & Stewardship Workflows

* **Fiscal-Year Giving & Pledge Insights:** View a clear breakdown of funds by fiscal year right on the Finance Dashboard, browse available fiscal years on the Year-End Giving page, and run a new **Fiscal-Year Pledge Comparison Report** to track giving trends over time.
* **Sample Giving Statement Preview:** Preview how your giving statement PDFs look using fictional sample data before generating real statements for your congregation.
* **Faster Payment & Check Entry:** Record check payments without needing a check number when it isn't required, and find families faster when looking up envelope numbers during payment entry.
* **Mobile-Friendly Deposit Charts:** The deposit editor funds chart now collapses neatly on smaller screens, and deposit dashboard charts display dates in proper chronological order.
* **Organized Statement Settings:** Statement Settings have moved under **Finance Admin**, keeping all giving configuration in one predictable place.

---

## 📱 Navigation, Mobile & Everyday Usability

* **Compact Icon Sidebar & Better Mobile Menus:** Keep navigation within easy reach on desktop with a collapsible icon sidebar that centers your workspace, while tablet and phone menus now open smoothly and stay reliably in view.
* **Clean, Consistent Popups & Notifications:** Replaced browser popups with clean, modern confirmations and toast notifications across the system.
* **Custom Logo Polish on Sign-In:** When your church uploads a custom logo, the redundant church name text is hidden on the login screen for a cleaner welcome.
* **Faster Large Carts & Maps:** Loading large member carts is noticeably faster, and storing individual coordinates means group and cart maps load right away without repeatedly looking up addresses.
* **Accurate Mailing Labels:** Inactive member records are now automatically excluded when generating mailing labels.

---

## 📧 Communication & Member Engagement

* **Member Portal Verification Links:** Family verification emails now link directly into the Member Portal with clearer step-by-step instructions for households.
* **Unified Event Emailing:** Clicking "Email All" from an event now opens the shared email composer, giving you the same reliable queue, formatting, and history tracking as other congregational messages.
* **Church Name in 2FA Apps:** Setting up two-factor authentication now displays your church's name inside authenticator apps, making it easy to tell accounts apart.

---

## 📅 Calendar, Dates & Global Localization

* **No Duplicate Calendar Events:** Events pinned to multiple calendars now appear once rather than cluttering your view.
* **Consistent Date Formats & Time Zones:** Dates across the app follow your church's configured date-picker format, CSV exports respect your church's time zone, and holidays appear on the right calendar day regardless of time zone differences.
* **Localized Holidays & PDFs:** Holiday calendar titles now display in your active language, and localized PDF font settings on the admin dashboard work reliably.
