# 🌸 ChurchCRM 7.6.0 — The "Automated Care & Everyday Usability" Release

**Release Date**: August 9, 2026
**Theme**: Automated Member Care, Smarter Financials, and Streamlined Workflows

**Version 7.6.0** delivers major updates designed to automate routine congregation outreach, introduce smart display options, streamline financial management, improve interface usability, and maintain our commitment to background system safety. From automated birthday greetings to auto light/dark themes and flexible pledge splits, this update keeps your day-to-day ministry workflows efficient and reliable.

---

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🎂 Automated Birthday Outreach
Staying connected with your congregation on their special day is now completely hands-free.
* **Automated Well-Wishes:** ChurchCRM can now automatically send birthday greeting emails to members on their birthday, ensuring every congregation member feels remembered and valued without extra staff effort.

### 🌓 Auto Theme Mode
* **Device-Matched Display Mode:** ChurchCRM now includes a brand-new **Auto** theme setting that automatically matches your computer or mobile phone's light or dark mode preferences seamlessly.

---

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 💰 Flexible Financial Tools & Clearer Giving Insights
Managing pledges, deposits, and contribution trends is simpler and more intuitive for your finance team.
* **Multi-Fund Pledge Split:** The redesigned Pledge Editor lets you split a single pledge across multiple funds seamlessly, simplifying multi-project capital campaigns and specialized giving.
* **Contributor Drill-Down Views:** Financial officers can click directly into any fund summary to see detailed contributor histories at a glance.
* **Clean Financial Tables:** Pledge summaries and deposit payment tables feature modern, clutter-free layouts that respect your site's custom local currency settings.
* **Strict Module Visibility:** Finance and Fundraiser controls are strictly gated by administrative settings, keeping non-financial roles focused exclusively on their relevant workspace.

### 👥 Usability & Directory Enhancements
We have eliminated common interface friction points across group management, reporting, and site navigation.
* **Streamlined Group Role Management:** Creating, editing, and assigning group roles now takes place in clean pop-up windows with built-in delete confirmation prompts to prevent accidental changes.
* **Reliable Member Verification Links:** When sending profile verification requests to families, error messages are clearer, and staff can preview confirmation emails before sending.
* **Accurate Custom Field Exports:** Custom fields in member list exports (CSV/PDF) now display true field names and values instead of raw background codes.
* **Polished Interface Navigation:** We removed duplicate action buttons on the People Dashboard and fixed dropdown menus so they open smoothly without getting clipped by table borders.
* **Interactive Church Location Setup:** Easily pinpoint your church's physical location with a new "Generate Coordinates" button and live map preview directly inside church information settings.
