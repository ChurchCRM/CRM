# 🔑 ChurchCRM 7.8.3: The "Open Doors" Release

**Release Date**: November 5, 2026
**Theme**: Member sign-in by email and passkey, giving statements that are easier to run, every PDF report on one engine, safer upgrades

Your members can now sign in to the Member Portal with their own email address, and your finance team has one clear place to start year-end giving statements. Reports, labels and name tags now share one PDF engine that follows your church's letterhead and language.

---

## ⚠️ Before You Upgrade

* **Member Portal sign-in changes on upgrade day.** Members sign in at `/portal/login` with a code or link emailed to them. The existing self-service logins stop working, and members no longer appear under **Admin → Users**, which now lists staff only. Email must be working on your server. Review **Admin → Member Portal** before you upgrade.
* **Staff sign in at `/admin`.** The old `/session/begin` address still works.
* **Members who share an email address** cannot be given portal sign-in until each person has their own address. The error names the people who share it.
* **The stock letterhead image is retired.** If your church never chose a letterhead, PDFs print your church name and address block. To use a logo, upload one under **Reports → Admin → Report Settings**. A letterhead path you typed yourself keeps working.
* **PHP 8.4 or newer** is required. If you host ChurchCRM on your own server, keep PHP patched.

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🔑 Members Sign In With Their Own Email
Members no longer need a username or a password.
* **Email code or link:** A member enters their email and ChurchCRM sends a code and a link. Links last 2 hours, and sessions end after 30 idle minutes.
* **Passkeys and remembered devices:** After the first sign-in, a member can add a passkey or stay signed in on their own device. Nothing is remembered unless they ask.
* **Sign-in and devices page:** Members see and remove their own devices.
* **Control for staff:** Turn sign-in on or off for any person, see who can sign in and when they last did, and view the audit trail (**Admin → Member Portal**).
* **Login as Person:** An administrator can view a member's portal from **People → [Name] → Actions**. It is read-only by default and every action is on the record. Staff accounts cannot be opened this way.
* **Email belongs to people:** The family email moves to the head of household or spouse, and the printed directory shows each person's own email.
* **Your language:** Members see the portal in the language they chose.

### 💰 Year-End Giving and Giving Statements
Your treasurer gets a starting point for the year's statements.
* **Year-End Giving page (Finance → Year-End Giving):** Pick a year, review families, payments and totals, work through a checklist (open deposits, payments not on a deposit, gifts with no family, church info), then preview, print or download.
* **Giving Statements page:** Choose the filters, preview the families and total in a popup, then download the PDF or CSV. Large churches can add every family without hitting a limit.
* **My Giving in the portal:** The head of household and spouse download their household's statement, with check numbers and the non-deductible amount shown.
* **Statement Settings (Finance → Admin):** Statement wording, signer, church tax ID and PDF handling in one auto-saving page. Finance users can use it without full administrator rights.

### 🖨️ One Letterhead, Uploaded in One Place
**Reports → Admin → Report Settings** lets you upload, preview and remove your church letterhead, and set the shared letter greeting and signature. Every PDF letter and statement uses it, and every family's statement in a combined PDF now starts with the letterhead.

### 📅 Repeat on the Nth Weekday
Recurring events can repeat on the "2nd Tuesday", in the Repeat Events editor and in a ministry's **New recurring event** dialog. Months without a 5th such weekday get no event.

## 🔒 Security & Sign-In

* **Staff sign-in is throttled:** Repeated wrong passwords from one address are slowed, and a locked account unlocks itself after 15 minutes (an administrator can still unlock it at once). Sign-in and password-reset pages answer the same way for every account, so they no longer reveal which usernames exist.
* **Password reset limits:** An account gets at most three reset emails an hour, and only the newest link works.
* **Custom fields stay private:** People who may not see a custom field no longer see it in search, the printed directory or the verification email PDF, and saving a record no longer erases values they could not see.
* **Voting Members report needs the Finance permission.**
* **The only administrator cannot be demoted:** The user editor refuses to remove the last administrator who can sign in.
* **FrankenPHP and Caddy installs:** The example Caddy configuration now blocks logs, hidden files, member photos and non-image files in `Images/`. Apache and nginx installs were not affected.
* **Restored backups:** Only known image file types are restored into the images folder.
* **Photo book needs the right role:** The Sunday School photo book is limited to group managers.
* **Email taken by two people:** Member Portal sign-in cannot be switched on for a person whose personal email is shared.

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 🖨️ Reports, Labels and Name Tags
* **One PDF engine:** The Church Directory, Confirm Report, Pledge Reminder, Family Pledge Summary, Group Report, Envelope Report, Zero Givers, Fundraiser sheets, Deposit reports, Sunday School class list and attendance sheet, and all label sheets now use the same PDF engine. They follow your language and your church letterhead, and filenames start with your church name.
* **Deposit Reports (Finance → Reports):** Review a deposit or a date range, preview the totals, download PDF or CSV, and print the bank slip separately.
* **Labels that line up:** Labels are placed at fixed positions on the sheet, long text stays inside its label, and bulk-mail ZIP codes keep their leading zeros.
* **New name tags:** Church name on top, a large first name beside the person's photo, and long names shrink to fit.
* **Group/Sunday School photo book:** Four photos per row, with age, birthday and parent phone numbers, and initials when there is no photo. Each class starts on a new page, and the class page has a Reports menu with one-click buttons on the dashboard.
* **Financial Reports in the Finance menu** for faster access.

### 👥 Member Portal
* **Take a selfie or crop a photo:** The portal profile uses the staff photo uploader, with crop, rotate, camera and iPhone photo support.
* **Cancelled volunteer dates:** My Schedule shows a Cancelled badge and a short note, and the home card no longer offers a cancelled date as your next commitment.
* **Spacing and badges:** Portal controls are spaced evenly and the "You" badge is readable.

### ⚙️ Administration
* **Safer database upgrades:** Upgrades now run one file per page load with a progress page, so a slow server is less likely to time out. A partly applied 7.8.1 email change can be retried safely.
* **Faster for everyone:** Pages no longer wait on background email, and slow map lookups no longer freeze your other tabs. Each request now holds one database connection instead of two.
* **Translations show on every server:** Church servers without the matching system locale now show the translated text instead of English.
* **Anonymous, opt-in usage statistics:** If you opt in, ChurchCRM can tell fresh installs from upgrades. Upgrade failures are reported without church or member data.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

* **Birthdays show the right age:** On their birthday a person showed a year younger, and the birthday email never mentioned the age. Both are fixed, and a birth year in the future shows no age.
* **Two-factor setup works again:** Enrolling in two-factor sign-in no longer fails when you confirm the code.
* **Admin menu and Sunday School chart:** The admin menu and the birthday chart filter no longer throw errors.
* **Email-change notice** shows the right names and addresses in every language.
* **Kiosk registration** no longer closes early on the day clocks go back.
