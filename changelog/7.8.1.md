# ChurchCRM 7.8.1 Release Notes

ChurchCRM 7.8.1 is built to lighten the administrative load on church staff, volunteer coordinators, and ministry leaders. This release brings key improvements to tracking students, managing ministry teams, communicating with your congregation, and keeping financial records accurate.

---

## 🏫 Student & Children’s Ministry Workflows

* **Simplified Grade Tracking:** Easily assign and track student grade levels (Pre-K through 12th grade) with built-in options tailored to different educational systems (US, UK, Australia, South Africa, and more).
* **Automated Annual Promotion:** Move students up to the next grade automatically at the end of the school year, eliminating the manual effort of updating every child's record one by one.
* **Connected Sunday School Rosters:** Student grade levels now flow automatically into member profiles, family records, Sunday School rosters, and attendance reports.

---

## 🤝 Volunteer & Team Coordination

* **Empowered Team Leaders:** Ministry leaders can now view, organize, and invite active members to their team pools directly from their dashboard.
* **Fewer, Better Notifications:** Volunteer coordinators receive a single daily digest summarizing unfilled positions across upcoming events, replacing frequent individual alert emails.
* **Easier Roster Management:** Roster headers remain visible while scrolling through volunteer schedules, making it easier to view large events.
* **Smoother Child Check-In:** The check-in kiosk now displays guardian photos, tracks who checked children in or out, updates seamlessly in the background, and fully supports multi-language congregations.

---

## 📧 Congregational Communication

* **Uninterrupted Group Emailing:** Large email blasts now send smoothly in the background, allowing you to keep working without waiting for screens to load or connection timeouts.
* **Clear Sending Status:** View real-time progress while drafting emails, with automatic retry tools to ensure failed messages reach your members.
* **Streamlined Menus:** Communication tools are now consolidated under the **People** menu, keeping messaging options right where you look for congregation details.

---

## 🔒 Financial Integrity & Security

* **Precise Donation Tracking:** Online gifts, checks, and cash entries are assigned distinct tracking codes, ensuring individual payments are never accidentally merged or overwritten during batch updates.
* **Protected Member Records:** Administrative rules prevent accidental account lockouts and ensure only authorized staff can modify member access.
* **Reliable Address Records:** Saving member updates preserves state and country details accurately without clearing location mappings or resetting drop-down menus.
* **Easier-to-Read Dark Mode:** Updated high-contrast visual styling ensures deposit tools, event schedules, and charts remain clear and readable in Dark Mode.
