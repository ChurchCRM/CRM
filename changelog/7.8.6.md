# ChurchCRM 7.8.6 Release Notes

**Release Date**: November 21, 2026
**Theme**: An installer that explains itself, Canadian giving receipts, redesigned record pages, and a proper front door for sign-in

The most common story we hear from new churches is that getting ChurchCRM installed was the hardest part — and our own numbers say the usual dead end is the database step, where a typing mistake used to end in an error nobody could act on. This release rebuilds that step so it explains itself. It also delivers official giving receipts for Canadian churches ahead of year-end, gives the person and family pages a fresh, consistent look, and puts one clear front door on sign-in.

---

## ⚠️ Before You Upgrade

**Check your database version, as with every 7.8 release.**

ChurchCRM needs one of these:

- MySQL **8.4** or newer
- MariaDB **10.11** or newer

Older ones no longer get security fixes from their makers. The upgrade checks for you: if your database is too old, the upgrade stops and changes nothing. Not sure what your site uses? Ask your hosting company before you upgrade.

**One change:** the old Volunteer Opportunities pages (the original "V1" tools) are retired in this release — and your data came with you. Volunteer assignments recorded on those pages were migrated to the current volunteer pages in an earlier release, which is where you will find them now.

**One automatic step:** during the upgrade, the family and person tables are converted to a modern text format so names with accents and emoji store and display correctly. It runs on its own; on a very large database it can add a few minutes to the upgrade.

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🛠️ Installing ChurchCRM explains itself now
The setup wizard is one page. When you click Install, ChurchCRM tests your database connection first — before anything is saved — and if something is wrong, it says what is wrong in plain language and what to do about it, instead of leaving you on a broken page. The database step was where most new installs died; it now helps you through.
* **Where:** any fresh install.

### 🍁 Giving receipts, cleaner for every charity — official receipts for Canada
There is now one place for your charity's own details — its name, address, and registration information, plus an uploaded signature — and your giving receipts and statements draw from it instead of scattering those details across settings. For Canadian churches, that same setup produces official donation receipts that carry what Canada requires, wording included.
* **Where:** Finance → charity settings, then giving statements and receipts as usual.

### 🚪 One front door for sign-in
Sign-in now opens on one clear page that sends each person the right way: members to the Member Portal, staff to the staff side, and new visitors to create an account when your church allows it. Churches with only one audience skip straight through — no extra click.
* **Where:** your ChurchCRM sign-in address.

### 👥 Person and family pages, redesigned
The two pages your staff live on have a new, matching look: a clear header, tabs across the top, and notes in their own tab, so finding a phone number, a note, or a giving history takes the same steps on a person as on a family.
* **Where:** any person or family record.

### 📊 One dashboard, your way
The dashboard brings its views together behind tabs, with date ranges you choose and paging through long lists, so the morning glance and the month-end review live on the same page.
* **Where:** Dashboard.

## 🔒 Security & Sign-In

* **Require two-factor sign-in, person by person:** An administrator can now require two-factor sign-in for a specific staff account — the volunteer who only checks in kids does not need the same rule as the treasurer, and now does not get it.
* **Password strength, everywhere:** The strength meter now appears on every password page — staff, reset, and the Member Portal — and it follows the minimum length your church set, instead of a one-size guess.

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 💰 Giving & Financial Tracking
* **Pledges that repeat:** A pledge can now be a recurring commitment, and it follows through the finance pages the way a one-time pledge does — no more re-creating the same pledge by hand.
* **Deposit chart, one line per fund:** The dashboard deposit chart draws a separate line for each fund, so a building campaign and the general fund stop blending into one curve.
* **Cleaner deposit closing:** Failed payments are cleared out before a card or a draft deposit is closed, and the payment defaults you set stay with each deposit slip instead of wandering to the next one.

### 📋 Reports
* **Directory Report remembers:** The report opens with the options you used last time, long option lists no longer break the page, and your uploaded letterhead now prints on the directory cover.
* **Envelope report that matches:** The envelope report now sorts and classifies the same way the Envelope Manager does, so the two stop disagreeing.

### 👥 People, Groups, and Events
* **Deleting a record is a clear, guided step:** Removing a person or family walks you through what will happen; records with giving, attendance, or notes stay protected, as before.
* **Notes open where you are:** Editing a note or a "why they came" opens in a popup on the page you are already on — no trip to a separate editor and back — with full rich-text editing restored.
* **Pending registrations stay out of mailings:** People who registered themselves and are still waiting for approval no longer slip into cart emails and mailing labels.
* **Filters can exclude:** The people list filters gained an Exclude switch, so "everyone except this group" is one search instead of a workaround.
* **Calendars list active people:** Annual calendars no longer carry inactive records.
* **Portal invites that can land:** Portal invitations go only to family members who can actually sign in, and the portal home no longer shows an empty family card to people without a family.
* **Everyone sees when an update is waiting:** The update notice now reaches all staff, and a read-only releases page shows anyone on staff what version you are on and what came before.
* **Small things, everywhere:** An icon on every sidebar item, tighter menus, readable contrast in the light theme, and last name first in CSV rollups.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

* **Kiosk guest cleanup blocked again:** Checked-in walk-in guests could not be removed from the kiosk after attendance was taken. Fixed for good — check-in no longer writes the note that blocked cleanup, and existing notes are removed by the upgrade.
* **Duplicate email history:** A person's timeline could record the same email twice. Fixed.
* **Dismissed notices returning:** A notification with a long ID could come back after you dismissed it. Fixed.
* **Property menus opening the wrong place:** Some property menu links were read by the browser as a web address and went nowhere useful. Fixed.
* **Custom field deletes failing quietly:** Removing a custom field could fail without saying so, or break on some databases. It now works, and says so when it cannot.
