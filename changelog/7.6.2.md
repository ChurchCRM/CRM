# 🌸 ChurchCRM 7.6.2 — The "Performance & Role Autonomy" Release

**Release Date**: August 25, 2026
**Theme**: Faster Page Loading, Role Autonomy, and Custom Search Fixes

**Version 7.6.2** brings important underlying performance upgrades, refined user permissions, and directory search fixes to keep your daily church administrative workflows fast, reliable, and secure.

---

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 🗝️ Expanded Role Autonomy
We’ve streamlined how module permissions work so team leaders can manage their areas without needing full administrative rights.
* **Independent Finance & Group Access:** Staff with Finance or Group Management roles now enjoy complete module access and tools within their respective areas without requiring full System Administrator privileges.

### ⚡ Faster System Performance & Database Speed
We've optimized key database lookups behind the scenes to make browsing member records and navigating dashboards even faster.
* **Database Indexing:** Added targeted database indexes on frequently accessed relational fields, resulting in noticeably quicker page loads and smoother navigation during peak office hours.

### 🔍 Search & Directory Reliability
* **Custom Search Query Fix:** Resolved an issue where running Custom Searches produced database errors, ensuring complex directory filtering works reliably.

### ⚙️ Administration & Ongoing Platform Safety
As with every release, we continually work behind the scenes to keep your software safe, stable, and secure.
* **Continuous Protections:** Applied unified code safety controls and updated background libraries to ensure your church's workspace remains safe and protected.

---

## 🌍 Global Language Polish
* **Complete Localization:** The system is fully localized across 45+ supported languages for all the new features and updates in this release.
