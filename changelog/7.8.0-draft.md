**Release Date**: TBD
**Theme**: Member Portal, Volunteer Scheduling, Your Church's Logo, Fund Categories and Tax Statements, Safer Sign-in

Updating a phone number shouldn't need a call to the church office, and finding a volunteer for Sunday shouldn't mean a round of texts. In 7.8.0 members can keep their own details up to date in a new Member Portal, and ministry leaders can schedule volunteers in one place. The office also gets a separate mailing address for families, labels for every People Report, and fund categories for churches with many funds. Please read **Before You Upgrade** first.

---

## ⚠️ Before You Upgrade

* **Custom Queries are removed.** The Data & Reports **Queries** page is gone, and the upgrade deletes the stored queries, including any your church wrote. The nine people queries now live at **People → Reports**. The two pledge queries are retired. Export what you need before you upgrade.
* **Admin API addresses moved.** If you wrote your own scripts against ChurchCRM's admin-only API, update them. The old addresses return 404.
* **Back up your database, as with every upgrade.** The upgrade wizard runs this release's database scripts for you.

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Member Portal
Members get a simple page of their own, so the office isn't the only way to update a family's details.
* **Who gets it:** a user whose only permission is **Edit Self** lands in the portal after signing in, instead of the admin screens. Grant **Edit Self** from the User editor. Staff see a **Member Portal** link in their user menu.
* **Profile and family:** members edit their own name, email, phones and photo. Adults in a family also edit the family's address, phone, email and wedding date, and can confirm "my details are correct". These changes save right away without staff review, and each one is recorded as a Note on the person.
* **Add a family member:** an adult can propose a new person for their family. The new person waits on **People → Self Registrations** until staff approve them, one at a time or in bulk.
* **Calendar:** members see the church calendar, choose which calendars to show, and subscribe to a personal feed in their own calendar app. You choose which calendars are shared in **Admin → Member Portal**. On an upgraded install no calendar is shared until you pick one.
* **Email history:** members see the emails ChurchCRM sent to them.
* **Your church's look:** an admin can switch the portal's theme in **Admin → Member Portal**. A theme is a folder copied onto your server, and it needs no coding.

### 📝 Self Registrations Review
New people who sign themselves up no longer slip into your records unnoticed. **People → Self Registrations** is now a review queue for the whole workflow, from sign-up to approval. It covers people who register themselves, family members proposed from the Member Portal, and walk-in guests registered at a kiosk.
* **What lands in the queue:** families and people who register themselves, family members proposed from the Member Portal, and walk-in guests registered at a kiosk. Each one carries a **needs review** flag until staff approve it.
* **Seen where staff already work:** the flag shows on people and family lists and views and on the main dashboard, so a pending record isn't mistaken for a verified one.
* **Approve one or many:** staff with permission to edit records approve a person or a family on its own, or approve a batch at once. A family member proposed from the portal is approved separately from their family.
* **Kiosk walk-in guests:** someone who isn't in the event's group can be checked in without leaving the kiosk. Staff tap **Register Guest** and enter a first and last name, plus a phone number or an email address. Birth date is optional. The guest is checked in at once, appears under Checked In with a **Guest** badge, and stays there after the screen refreshes. Their timeline records that they registered at the kiosk and were checked in, and approving the record in the review keeps them checked in.
* **Only for a real, open event:** the **Register Guest** button appears only when the event has a linked group and check-in is open, from one hour before the start until the end.
* **Guest classification:** an admin chooses the classification given to kiosk guests in the **Kiosk Settings** card on the Kiosk Manager page. It defaults to *Guest*.
* **Know what to expect:** after the upgrade, existing self-registered records that were never edited show as needing review, so the first look at the queue may be long. Approving is the only action for now, with no Reject or Delete button in the review.

### 🙋 Volunteer Management v2
A new way to schedule who serves when, built on your existing calendar, events and groups. It is **off by default**. Choose **V2** or **Both** in **Admin → Ministry Settings** when you're ready. Your existing volunteer data is untouched.
* **Ministries and teams:** each ministry has its own volunteers, events and teams. A team can be linked to a Sunday School class, and its Teacher role stays in step.
* **Positions and qualifications:** record who is cleared to fill which role. Revoking a qualification keeps the history.
* **Schedules that fill themselves:** a schedule follows a class's meetings, a ministry's own events, or church events of a type, and creates each service slot ahead of time. Regular volunteers are placed automatically while they stay qualified.
* **Clear staffing:** every screen says plainly where you stand: "Needs 2 more", "Covered · 1 more welcome", "Full · 2 of 2".
* **Volunteers answer for themselves:** in the portal they accept or decline, propose a substitute, sign up for open slots, or offer to help a ministry. Team leaders manage their own team from **My Teams**.
* **Right access for each role:** volunteer managers, ministry coordinators and team leaders each see only what they run.
* **Email that goes out on its own:** assignment, sign-up, decline, open-slot, swap, help-offer and reminder emails. Reminders go out 48 hours ahead by default, and 0 turns them off. **Reminders and email delivery depend on your server's scheduled task (cron job) running.** **Admin → Ministry Settings** suggests a schedule.

### 💰 Giving & Financial Tracking
Treasurers get better ways to organize funds and to get statements to families.
* **Donation Fund Categories:** churches with many funds can group them under categories such as "Missions" or "Seasonal". The groups show on the Finance Dashboard, the Pledge Dashboard and the fund filter on reports. **Finance → Donation Funds** also lets you rename or delete a category, and activate or deactivate a fund from its row menu. Categories are optional, and deleting one never deletes a fund.
* **Tax statements by year:** the family **Finance** menu has a **Tax Documents** list with **Print** and **Email** for each year of giving. **Financial Settings** controls how many years are listed (5 by default, 0 for no limit).
* **Faster deposit entry:** in the payment editor, **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘+Enter on a Mac) saves and starts the next payment. The buttons lock while a save is running, so a double click can't enter a payment twice.
* **Pledge Dashboard layout:** the per-fund cards are replaced by four totals.

### 🖼️ Your Church's Logo
ChurchCRM can now look like your church. An administrator uploads a logo in **Admin → System → Church Information**, and no files need to be copied onto the server. Remove it at any time to go back to the ChurchCRM logo.
* **What to upload:** a JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP image. SVG is not accepted. A wide banner, about 3.5 times wider than tall, fits best. Large images are shrunk to fit 1200 x 400 and are never enlarged, and transparency is kept.
* **Where it appears on screen:**
  * The sidebar, where it replaces the ChurchCRM logo in both light and dark themes and hides the church name text beside it.
  * The sign-in, password reset, two-factor, change password and error pages.
  * The error page on shared calendar links.
  * The header of the Member Portal.
  * The top of every email ChurchCRM sends. If you haven't uploaded a logo, emails still use the optional fallback logo address (`sChurchLogoURL`) from System Settings.
* **Where it does not appear:**
  * **Printed output.** Printed and PDF reports and letters (such as the church directory) still use the church letterhead image from **Report Settings**, not the uploaded logo. Uploading a logo doesn't change anything you print.
  * The family registration, family verification and public calendar pages, the "page not found" pages, and the setup wizard keep showing your church name or the ChurchCRM name as text.
* **Your server:** the logo is saved as `Images/church-logo.png`, so that folder must be writable. Upgrades and release checks leave the file alone.

### 📬 Family Addresses & Maps
Mail goes to the mailing address, and maps find the home.
* **A separate mailing address:** a family can record an optional second address and tick **This is the mailing address**. Labels, letters, reminders, tax statements and fundraiser statements use it. Maps, directions and "find neighbours" keep using the primary address. An unticked second address is shown for information only.
* **More US addresses found on the map:** in **Admin → People Settings → Map Settings** you can add the US Census Bureau's address lookup as a fallback after the default one. It is optional, and nothing changes unless you pick it.
* **Route numbers stay intact:** "FM 1960" is no longer turned into "1960th" before the address is looked up.

### 🏷️ Print Labels from Every People Report
Each People Report has a **Print Labels** button next to **Add All to Cart** and **Download CSV**. It prints that report's own rows, with its current filters, without using the cart. **Birthdays & Anniversaries** prints both sets in one run, with a label for each birthday and one per couple for anniversaries.

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 💰 Giving & Financial Fixes
* **Emailing a tax statement** no longer stops with a blank error page.
* **Pledge Reminders for cash payments:** the report no longer fails for a family with a payment that has no check number.
* **Giving History totals** now use your church's currency format.
* **Editing an active fund** no longer switches it to inactive.

### 👥 Usability & Directory Enhancements
* **People Reports:** nine people reports with classification and month filters, now in **Reports → People Reports**.
* **Booklet-style directory:** **Directory Reports** has a **Page Layout** choice. **Folded Booklet** prints half-size pages two to a sheet. Print double-sided on the short edge and fold. "Number of Columns" is now "Columns per Page".
* **Directory order for mixed-name families:** a family is filed under the head of household's last name, so it appears under the right letter.
* **Groups:** **Change Role** now saves the new role. Deleting a role no longer moves other members into the wrong role. Group members sort by last name, then first name.
* **Reports menu:** it lists only the reports each user can open, with People Reports and Financial Reports laid out the same way.

### 🖥️ Kiosk
* **Kiosk check-in:** the counter no longer drops to 0 on refresh, and each person's status reflects the event the kiosk is assigned to.
* **Your church on the screen:** the event header shows the church name, and your uploaded logo when there is one.
* **Clearer wording:** an empty roster now says "No Group Members Found" instead of "No Class Members Found".

### 📷 Photos
* **Phone photos work:** photos up to 50 MB are resized in the browser before upload, and iPhone **HEIC** photos are converted to JPEG for you.
* **Photo upload:** the page reloads only after the server confirms the new photo.

### ⚙️ Administration & Ongoing Platform Safety
* **People Settings hub:** **Admin → People Settings** collects the People, Family, New Member, New Record Defaults (country, state, city, ZIP) and Map settings in one place. They save as you change them. These settings moved out of System Settings and Church Info, so look here if you can't find them.
* **Feature Toggles:** **Admin → System → Feature Toggles** turns modules on or off from one page, and the menu follows the switches, even for administrators.
* **Sign-in and sessions:**
  * Deceased and inactive people can no longer sign in. Any login tied to a person with a deceased or deactivated date stops working, including shared accounts and API keys, so check your accounts before you upgrade. You cannot mark the last administrator who can sign in as deceased or inactive.
  * A person with a login can be deleted only by an administrator, never your own login, and never the last administrator. The Delete buttons say why when they are disabled.
  * Your password details and 2FA secret are no longer kept in the session file on the server.
  * A half-finished two-factor sign-in expires after 10 minutes.
  * Idle sessions last until your configured timeout, instead of ending silently after about 24 minutes.
* **Fields stay private:** restricted custom fields are no longer shown to users without access. Family envelope and scan fields are visible only to users with Finance permission.
* **Admin-only API moved:** admin-only API calls now live under `/admin/api` and are covered by the administrator check on every route. The moved calls are person custom fields and properties, telemetry consent, volunteer opportunities and bulk geocoding.
* **Less information shared:** responses to connected tools are no longer cached by browsers, and visitors who aren't signed in can't see which ChurchCRM version you run.
* **Plugins:** plugin menu items respect their permission, and an OpenLP server address that points at an internal network address is refused.
* **Group Properties:** the menu entry now appears only for users who can open it.
* **Email log:** every email ChurchCRM sends is recorded with its recipient, subject and status. The message body is kept only for messages written in the email composer. Entries are kept indefinitely.
* **Better bug reports:** in-app issue reports include your locale and timezone.
* **Help links:** the Help menu opens a documentation search for the page you are on. Links from ChurchCRM to churchcrm.io and the docs carry a source tag, and no church, user or install data is sent.
* **Settings panel:** it stays locked until its values load, so a slow page can't save blanks over real values.
