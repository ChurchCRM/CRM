**Release Date**: TBD
**Theme**: Member Portal, Volunteer Management v2, Mailing Addresses, Smarter Giving Tools, Tighter Sign-in Security

Members can now keep their own details up to date, and ministry leaders can schedule volunteers without a spreadsheet. 7.8.0 adds a self-service Member Portal and a new volunteer scheduling system. Staff also get a separate mailing address for families, labels for every People Report, and fund categories for churches with many funds. Sign-in and permission checks are stricter, so please read **Before You Upgrade**.

---

## ⚠️ Before You Upgrade

* **Custom Queries are removed.** The Data & Reports **Queries** page is gone, and the upgrade deletes the stored queries, including any your church wrote. The nine people queries now live at **People → Reports** (`/people/reports`). The two pledge queries are retired. Export what you need before you upgrade.
* **Deceased and inactive people can no longer sign in.** Any login tied to a person with a deceased or deactivated date stops working, including shared accounts and API keys. Check your accounts first. You cannot mark the last administrator who can sign in as deceased or inactive.
* **Some admin API addresses moved, with no redirect.** These now live under `/admin/api` and the old addresses return 404:
  * `/api/system/custom-fields/person`
  * `/api/system/properties/person`
  * `/api/system/telemetry-consent`
  * `/api/volunteer-opportunities`
  * `/api/map/geocode-all`
* **Some settings moved.** People, Family, New Member, default address and Map settings are now in **Admin → People Settings**, and module on/off switches are in **Admin → System → Feature Toggles**.
* **`/v2/reports` is gone.** Use **Reports** in the menu.
* **Back up your database first.** This release adds several database upgrade scripts.

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Member Portal
Members get a simple page of their own, so they stop asking the office to change a phone number.
* **Who gets it:** a user whose only permission is **Edit Self** lands in the portal at `/portal` after sign-in, instead of the admin screens. Staff see a **Member Portal** link in their user menu. Grant **Edit Self** from the User editor.
* **Profile and family:** members edit their own name, email, phones and photo. Adults in a family also edit the family's address, phone, email and wedding date, and can confirm "my details are correct". Edits save immediately and are recorded as Notes on the person.
* **Add a family member:** an adult can propose a new person for their family. The new person waits on **People → Self Registrations** until staff approve them, one at a time or in bulk.
* **Calendar:** members see the church calendar, choose which calendars to show, and subscribe to a personal feed in their own calendar app. You choose which calendars are shared in **Admin → Member Portal**. On an upgraded install no calendar is shared until you pick one.
* **Email history:** members see the emails ChurchCRM sent to them.
* **Your church's look:** an admin can switch the portal's theme in **Admin → Member Portal**. Themes are folders on the server, with no code to write.

### 🙋 Volunteer Management v2
A new way to schedule who serves when, built on your existing calendar, events and groups. It is **off by default**: choose **V2** or **Both** in **Admin → Ministry Settings**. Your existing volunteer data is untouched.
* **Ministries and teams:** each ministry has its own volunteers, events and teams. A team can be linked to a Sunday School class, and its Teacher role stays in step.
* **Positions and qualifications:** record who is cleared to fill which role. Revoking a qualification keeps the history.
* **Schedules that fill themselves:** a schedule follows a class's meetings, a ministry's own events, or church events of a type, and creates each service slot ahead of time. Regular volunteers are placed automatically while they stay qualified.
* **Clear staffing:** every screen says plainly where you stand: "Needs 2 more", "Covered · 1 more welcome", "Full · 2 of 2".
* **Volunteers answer for themselves:** in the portal they accept or decline, propose a substitute, sign up for open slots, or offer to help a ministry. Team leaders manage their own team from **My Teams**.
* **Right access for each role:** volunteer managers, ministry coordinators and team leaders each see only what they run.
* **Email that goes out on its own:** assignment, sign-up, decline, gap, swap, help-offer and reminder emails. **Reminders and email delivery need the scheduled task to be running** (`cli/timerjobs.php`, every 15 minutes is suggested). Reminders go out 48 hours ahead by default, and 0 turns them off.

### 📬 A Separate Mailing Address for Families
A family can record an optional second address and tick **This is the mailing address**. Labels, letters, reminders, tax statements and fundraiser statements use it. Maps, directions and "find neighbours" keep using the primary address. An unticked second address is shown as informational only.

### 🏷️ Print Labels from Every People Report
Each People Report has a **Print Labels** button next to **Add All to Cart** and **Download CSV**. It prints that report's own rows, with its current filters, without using the cart. **Birthdays & Anniversaries** prints both sets, one label per person for birthdays and one per couple for anniversaries.

### 💰 Donation Fund Categories
Churches with many funds can group them under categories such as "Missions" or "Seasonal". The groups show on the Finance Dashboard, the Pledge Dashboard and the fund filter on reports. **Finance → Donation Funds** also lets you rename or delete a category, and activate or deactivate a fund from its row menu. Categories are optional.

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 💰 Giving & Financial Tracking
* **Tax statements by year:** the family **Finance** menu has a **Tax Documents** list with **Print** and **Email** for each year of giving. **Financial Settings** controls how many years are listed (5 by default, 0 for no limit). Emailing a statement no longer stops with a blank error page.
* **Faster deposit entry:** in the payment editor, **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘+Enter on a Mac) saves and starts the next payment, and the buttons lock while a save is running so a double click cannot post twice.
* **Pledge Dashboard layout:** the per-fund cards are replaced by four totals.
* **Pledge Reminders for cash payments:** the report no longer fails for a family with a payment that has no check number.
* **Giving History totals** now use your church's currency format.
* **Editing an active fund** no longer switches it to inactive.

### 👥 Usability & Directory Enhancements
* **Self Registrations review:** **People → Self Registrations** lists new sign-ups and proposed family members that need a look, with a needs-review flag on lists and views. Existing self-registered records that were never edited show as needing review after the upgrade, so expect a backlog the first time.
* **People Reports:** nine people reports with classification and month filters, now in **Reports → People Reports**.
* **Booklet-style directory:** **Directory Reports** has a **Page Layout** choice. **Folded Booklet** prints half-size pages two to a sheet; print double-sided on the short edge and fold. "Number of Columns" is now "Columns per Page".
* **Directory order for mixed-name families:** a family is filed under the head of household's last name, so it appears under the right letter.
* **Groups:** **Change Role** now saves the new role. Deleting a role no longer moves other members into the wrong role. Group members sort by last name, then first name.
* **Reports menu:** it lists only the reports each user can open, with People Reports and Financial Reports in the same layout.
* **Kiosk check-in:** the counter no longer drops to 0 on refresh, and each person's status reflects the assigned event.

### 📷 Photos & Church Logo
* **Church logo:** upload it in **Admin → System → Church Information**. It replaces the ChurchCRM logo in the sidebar, on the sign-in pages and in emails. The `Images/` folder must be writable.
* **Phone photos work:** photos up to 50 MB are resized in the browser before upload. iPhone **HEIC** photos are converted to JPEG for you.
* **Photo upload:** the page reloads only after the server confirms the new photo.

### 📍 Mapping & Geocoding Fixes
* **More US addresses found:** **Admin → People Settings → Map Settings** lets you add the **US Census** geocoder as a fallback after Nominatim. It is optional, and nothing changes unless you pick it.
* **Route numbers stay intact:** "FM 1960" is no longer turned into "1960th" before lookup.

### ⚙️ Administration & Ongoing Platform Safety
* **People Settings hub:** **Admin → People Settings** collects the People, Family, New Member, New Record Defaults (country, state, city, ZIP) and Map settings in one place. They save as you change them.
* **Feature Toggles:** **Admin → System → Feature Toggles** turns modules on or off from one page, and the menu follows the switches, even for administrators.
* **Sign-in and sessions:**
  * Deceased and inactive people cannot sign in.
  * A person with a login can be deleted only by an administrator, never your own login, and never the last administrator. The Delete buttons say why when they are disabled.
  * Sessions no longer store your password hash, API key or 2FA secret.
  * A half-finished two-factor sign-in expires after 10 minutes.
  * Idle sessions last until your configured timeout, instead of ending silently after about 24 minutes.
* **Fields stay private:** restricted custom fields are no longer shown to users without access. Family envelope and scan fields are visible only to users with Finance permission.
* **Fewer information leaks from the API:** API responses are not cached or content-sniffed, and anonymous callers no longer see the ChurchCRM version.
* **Plugins:** plugin menu items respect their permission, and an OpenLP server address pointing at an internal network address is refused.
* **Group Properties:** the menu entry now appears only for users who can open it.
* **Email log:** every email ChurchCRM sends is recorded with its recipient, subject and status. The body is kept only for composer messages. Entries are kept indefinitely.
* **Better bug reports:** in-app issue reports include your locale and timezone.
* **Help links:** the Help menu opens a documentation search for the page you are on. Links from ChurchCRM to churchcrm.io and the docs carry a source tag, and no church, user or install data is sent.
* **Settings panel:** it stays locked until its values load, so a slow page can't save blanks over real values.

### 📱 Mobile & Display Enhancements
* **Calendar and tables:** the calendar no longer loops when resized, calendar colours display correctly, and list tables have more consistent spacing.

## 🧰 Behind the Scenes
* **Security & Dependencies:** fixes for three reported vulnerabilities (a field-level access bypass, an SSRF in the OpenLP plugin and a stored XSS in the group menu), plus routine dependency updates.
