# 🌸 ChurchCRM 7.5.1 — The "Global Reach & Smarter Tools" Update

**Release Date**: July 26, 2026  
**Theme**: Built-In Email Composer, Universal Local Currency, and LTR/RTL Language Alignment

**Version 7.5.1** delivers major quality-of-life enhancements for your church's day-to-day operations. This update introduces a powerful built-in email composer, expands custom local currency formatting across all financial reports, overhauls the Fundraiser dashboard, and applies extensive structural fixes for global, non-Latin, and right-to-left (RTL) languages.

---

## ✉️ New In-App Email Composer
Say goodbye to character limits and broken external email links!
* **No More Cut-Off Recipient Lists:** Clicking email links across Member, Cart, and Group pages now opens a clean, built-in email composer instead of relying on external `mailto:` links. This eliminates the old 2,000-character link limit that previously broke mass emails for larger congregations.
* **One-Click Clipboard Copy:** Easily copy full recipient lists to your clipboard for use in external mail clients when sending large announcements.
* **Simplified Settings:** We’ve removed outdated "email delimiter" toggles to keep your user settings clean and clutter-free.

---

## 💱 Universal Currency Formatting Across All Reports
Building on the finance customization introduced in 7.5.0, your custom local currency settings now extend across the entire system.
* **Localized Financial Reports:** All generated PDF reports—including deposit slips, end-of-year tax statements, pledge summaries, and payment reminders—now automatically use your configured local currency symbol and decimal format instead of defaulting to hardcoded dollar signs (`$`).

---

## 🏛️ Revamped Fundraiser Management
Our fundraising tools have received a comprehensive user interface overhaul to give you better visibility into church projects.
* **Expanded Campaign Tracking:** Fundraiser campaigns now support end dates, status tracking, campaign types, target goal amounts, and direct links to specific accounting funds.
* **Campaign Details & Archives:** Enjoy a dedicated detail view for active campaigns, a new archive page for completed fundraisers, and an integrated "Fundraisers" system calendar to keep your community informed.

---

## 🌍 Global Language & Word-Order Precision
We've made significant investments in how ChurchCRM handles world languages, specifically addressing word-order rules for dynamic terms and non-Latin scripts.
* **SOV & Complex Grammar Fixes:** Resolved word-order defects in automated phrase consolidation (such as "Add New [Item]" or "Delete Confirmation"). Languages with Subject-Object-Verb (SOV) sentence structures—such as Japanese, Korean, and various non-Latin scripts—now render natural, grammatically correct action prompts.
* **RTL & Script Clean-up:** We stripped out embedded HTML fragments, dangling colons, and decorative punctuation from translation strings. This ensures smooth visual alignment across both Left-to-Right (LTR) and Right-to-Left (RTL) languages without broken layout containers.
* **42 Localized Locales:** Updated POEditor translation sweeps across all supported languages ensure that new email composer alerts and fundraiser labels speak your team's native language.

---

## 🪲 Important Fixes & Enhancements
* **Accurate Member Classification Filters:** Fixed an indexing issue on the People Dashboard where filtering by classification skipped past the third option, ensuring your member directory lists remain completely accurate.
* **Smooth Upgrade Wizard:** Resolved an intermittent issue where stray server notices could cause the upgrade wizard to show a false "upgrade failed" alert even when the update completed successfully.
* **Embeddable Public Calendars:** External calendar sharing links can now be seamlessly embedded within third-party church website frames.
* **Polished Interface Dropdowns:** Fixed visual menu clipping on group member tables and event lists so options no longer get cut off at the edge of the screen.

---
