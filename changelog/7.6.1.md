# 🌸 ChurchCRM 7.6.1 — The "Upgrade Experience & Financial Precision" Release

**Release Date**: August 16, 2026
**Theme**: Redesigned Upgrade Wizard, Universal Financial Formatting, and Refined Navigation

**Version 7.6.1** delivers major usability enhancements to streamline software updates, complete multi-phase currency localization across all charts and reports, and eliminate minor interface friction points. 

---

## ✨ Exciting New Features

### 🚀 Redesigned Upgrade Wizard
Upgrading ChurchCRM is now clearer and more transparent than ever.
* **Modern Release Insights:** The "What You'll Gain" step has been completely redesigned with a stacked, easy-to-read layout that highlights the latest release notes and improvements before you apply an update.
* **Cleaner Advanced Mode:** Simplified advanced settings keep technical details organized without cluttering the main upgrade flow.

---

## 🛠️ Enhancements & Improvements

### 💰 Comprehensive Financial & Fund Management
Building on our financial overhaul, local currency formatting and donation tracking are now seamless across all screens.
* **Universal Currency Formatter:** All financial data—including interactive dashboard charts, report tables, per-row transaction amounts, and generated PDF statements—now consistently reflect your configured local currency symbol and decimal formatting.
* **Refactored Donation Fund Editor:** Managing pledge funds and donation categories is faster, more reliable, and fully integrated into the modern finance module.
* **Open Deposit Indicators:** A new badge on the main Finance navigation menu displays the count of open deposits at a glance, helping staff keep track of pending accounting tasks.
* **Fundraiser Widget Precision:** Corrected calculation discrepancies on the Fundraiser dashboard widget to ensure stat counts align accurately with campaign totals.

### 👥 Usability & Navigation Polish
We have eliminated common display issues across menus, carts, and report tools.
* **Cart to Group Routing:** Fixed an issue where opening the Cart to Group tool with an empty selection prevented group and role dropdowns from displaying.
* **Clean & Consistent Icons:** Restored missing navigation, PDF export, and shopping cart icons across all headers and tables for a uniform visual experience.
* **Improved Badge Layouts:** Adjusted birthday and anniversary badge text to display cleanly on a second line, preventing text overlap on member profile cards.
* **Smarter Report Queries:** Resolved blank selection displays and fixed report generation for wedding anniversary queries.

### ⚙️ Administration & Ongoing Platform Safety
As with every release, we continually work behind the scenes to keep your software safe, stable, and secure.
* **Calendar Sidebar Translations:** Public and private calendar names in the sidebar now automatically adapt to your active language settings.
* **Continuous Protections:** Routine maintenance, secure CSV data handling, and input protections have been applied across forms and imports to keep your church's workspace safe and reliable.

## 🌍 Global Language Polish
* **Complete Localization:** The system is fully localized across 45+ supported languages for all the new features and updates in this release.
